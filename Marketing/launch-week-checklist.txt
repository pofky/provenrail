WHAT THE AUDIENCE PUNISHES, so do not do it

  Do not open by arguing that Claude Code cannot stop destructive commands. It can, auto mode
  is the default on Pro, Max and Team, and the first reply will say so. Every draft here
  concedes that in its first paragraph on purpose.
  Do not claim the guard is a security boundary. It is a guardrail for a trusted agent that
  makes mistakes, and saying otherwise invites the bypasses that killed similar launches.
  Do not post a landing page. Show HN requires something people can run.
  Do not defend. A named bypass is a gift; take it, test it, ship it, say thank you.
  Do not mention Codex, Gemini CLI, Copilot or Cursor as supported. They are not driven.
