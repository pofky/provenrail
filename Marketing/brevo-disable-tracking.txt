Brevo dashboard, one setting to change.

Sign in at https://app.brevo.com, then go to Transactional, then Settings, then the Tracking section. Turn off click tracking and open tracking for transactional email. Save.

Why it matters: every Provenrail sign-in email currently has its link rewritten to a Brevo tracking domain (sendibt2.com) and carries an open-tracking pixel. That means Brevo logs when each user opens their sign-in email and when they click it, tied to their email address. A one-time authentication token should not travel through a third-party redirect, link rewriting to an unrelated domain is a phishing signal that some mail security products block, and we would rather not measure sign-in emails at all.
