# Provenrail UX Audit - 2026-08-18

Auditor: Claude (claude-sonnet-4-6). Scope: every page in web/ audited locally at 320px, 375px, 1280px via Playwright; production https://provenrail.com checked against the same pages. Where findings differ by environment, the label [PROD], [LOCAL], or [BOTH] says which.

Bar: what Linear, Stripe, Mercury, Arc, Raycast or Notion would ship in 2027. Design research reference: docs/design-research.md.

---

## TOP TEN - ordered by revenue / trust impact

### 1. start.html says "Provenrail can host the record" - contradicts the new anchoring model [PROD + LOCAL]

**File:** `web/start.html:332`, `web/start.html:370`, JSON-LD at `start.html:459`

The text at line 332 reads: "Provenrail can host the record behind a shareable proof link and add a trusted timestamp from an independent authority. That is the paid tier." Line 370 and the JSON-LD FAQ answer both say: "Data only leaves your machine if you later choose a hosted plan to share proof links."

The new pricing.html (local, today's change) says the opposite: "Provenrail hosts identity and billing only. Agent records live in your own sink, on your own infrastructure." The Builder plan feature now reads "Independent anchoring: send the root of your chain, **keep every record**."

This is a live factual inconsistency on the production /start page. A prospect who reads /start is told their records go to Provenrail; a prospect who reads /pricing is told they don't. One of them is wrong, and currently the one that will be trusted (the step-by-step guide) is the stale one.

**Fix:** Update start.html step 7 to describe the anchoring model: you send a Merkle root, not your records. "Provenrail receives only a cryptographic root over your record hashes, not the records themselves. That root is independently timestamped and publicly anchored. Your records never leave your machine." The JSON-LD answer needs the same update.

### 2. /for-agencies and /vs-microsoft-agt return 404 on production but are in sitemap.xml [PROD]

**File:** `web/sitemap.xml:100`, `web/sitemap.xml:106`

Playwright confirmed 404 on `https://provenrail.com/for-agencies` and `https://provenrail.com/vs-microsoft-agt`. Googlebot will crawl those URLs, receive 404, and mark them as unavailable. The sitemap lists them as canonical pages, signalling that they should exist. Every crawl that hits a 404 from the sitemap is a crawl-budget wasted on a page that should be content.

These are both substantive pages in the local repo, not stubs. The for-agencies page has a full scenario walkthrough, a proof receipt example, a quickstart, and FAQ. The vs-microsoft-agt page has a trust-boundary diagram, a comparison table, and the honest "AGT does real work" acknowledgement that builds credibility. Both are ready to ship.

**Fix:** Deploy both pages. If they cannot go live yet, remove them from sitemap.xml until they do.

### 3. Builder plan in index.html missing the two new anchoring features [LOCAL - will break on deploy]

**File:** `web/index.html:657-665` vs `web/pricing.html` Builder plan

The dedicated pricing page (local) lists these two Builder features that the homepage pricing section does not:
- "Independent anchoring: send the root of your chain, keep every record (pr anchor-push)"
- "A public anchor receipt your client or auditor can check without an account"

When the local changes are deployed, a visitor who lands on the homepage and evaluates the Builder plan will see a shorter, older feature list than a visitor who lands on /pricing. The JSON-LD structured data in index.html (line 116 Offer block for Builder) also does not mention these features.

**Fix:** Add both lines to the Builder `<ul class="plan-features">` in index.html. Update the JSON-LD Builder offer description to mention independent anchoring.

### 4. Nav is overcrowded at 1280px - anchor links pollute every page [BOTH]

**File:** `web/index.html` nav (12 items), all other pages (10 items)

Production screenshot confirmed: the homepage nav renders 12 items inline at 1280px: "How it works | Watch | Use cases | Why Provenrail | Pricing | FAQ | Compare | Start here | Docs | GitHub | Sign in | **Start free**". Non-homepage pages render 10 items.

Five of the homepage items (How it works, Watch, Use cases, Why Provenrail, FAQ) are `/#section` anchor links. From any page other than the homepage they navigate the user back to the homepage and then scroll. A user on /pricing who clicks "How it works" lands on the homepage hero with no clear return path. The items are also rendered at 0.9rem in narrow gaps - crammed, not considered.

Linear uses 5 nav items. Stripe uses 6. Mercury uses 4. 10-12 is twice the premium bar and reads as "every link someone added over time" rather than a deliberate information architecture.

**Fix:** Cut to: [Logo] -- How it works | Compare | Pricing | Docs -- [GitHub] [Sign in] [Start free]. Absorb "Watch", "Use cases", "Why Provenrail", "FAQ", "Start here" into a "How it works" dropdown or into the homepage sections they link to. Move "GitHub" to an icon-only link at the right edge.

### 5. Reveal animation is 700ms - reads as slow page not premium craft [BOTH]

**File:** `web/styles.css:121`

```css
.reveal { opacity: 0; transform: translateY(18px); transition: opacity .7s var(--ease), transform .7s var(--ease); }
```

Design research: "entrance animation 250-300ms; 150ms micro-interactions. Never ease-in for entrances. A stagger 150-200ms reads as a loading bug - 50ms max between list items, or it reads like a loading bug." The current 700ms is 2-3x the premium bar. On a fast connection every scroll causes a visible delay while content floats up. On slow connections it compounds the perceived wait.

The hero title itself uses this class. The user's first impression of the headline is a slow-motion arrival.

**Fix:** Change `.7s` to `.35s` on both opacity and transform. Add `transition-delay: .05s` at most between staggered sibling reveals. Consider removing `.reveal` from the hero title entirely - the hero should land immediately.

### 6. DM Sans is not distinctive and is missing font-optical-sizing [BOTH]

**File:** `web/styles.css:17-36` (font declarations), no `font-optical-sizing: auto` anywhere

DM Sans is declared as a variable font (weight 100-1000) but `font-optical-sizing: auto` is never set. At 4.6rem (the hero headline) the font renders with the same glyph proportions as at 0.9rem nav text. Variable fonts with optical sizing axes adjust the stroke contrast and spacing for the current display size. Not using it is leaving free legibility on the table.

More fundamentally: DM Sans is a free Google Font used in thousands of design templates. The design research is explicit - "plain default-Inter-everywhere is the slop tell" and DM Sans is one rung above Inter, not a distinctive choice. It reads as "developer chose a nice template font." Raycast customizes Inter with `font-feature-settings: 'ss03' 1` to break the generic look. DM Sans has `ss01` (alternate a) and `ss02` (alternate g) stylistic sets that would differentiate it from template use.

**Fix for now:** Add `font-optical-sizing: auto;` to the `html` or `body` rule. Add `font-feature-settings: 'ss01' 1;` to the body rule to activate the alternate single-story 'a'. For the hero title specifically, add `font-variation-settings: 'wdth' 100;` if you want tighter horizontal compression at large sizes.

**Fix for next milestone:** Evaluate a licensed or more distinctive typeface for display use. The design research points to Satoshi, General Sans, or Clash Display as viable premium open-source choices that do not read as templates.

### 7. for-agencies.html inline styles miss CSS variables that styles.css defines [LOCAL]

**File:** `web/for-agencies.html` inline `<style>` block (~line 30-90)

The page defines its own `:root` block with a subset of the CSS variables. Variables present in styles.css but absent from this page's inline block include: `--warn`, `--bad`, `--accent-glow-sm`, `--border-hi`, `--shadow-card`, `--ring`, `--radius`, `--radius-sm`, `--ease`, `--max-w`, `--gutter`. The footer and nav import styles.css, so any component that uses these variables will resolve to the dark-mode defaults (since the inline block only defines the core set) even when the user's system is in light mode.

Concrete risk: the `.plan-card` in the pricing section that the for-agencies page links to uses `--shadow-card` and `--radius`. If for-agencies.html ever includes a plan card component, it would render the dark shadow in light mode.

**Fix:** Remove the duplicate `:root` block from for-agencies.html. The page already imports styles.css; the variables are available. The inline block adds nothing except a source of drift. Do the same for vs-microsoft-agt.html.

### 8. for-agencies.html nav div is missing role="navigation" [LOCAL]

**File:** `web/for-agencies.html` nav links div (~line 196)

Every other page's nav dropdown div has `role="navigation"`. for-agencies.html has:
```html
<div id="nav-links" class="nav-links">
```
vs. the correct form everywhere else:
```html
<div id="nav-links" class="nav-links" role="navigation">
```

Screen readers expose navigation landmarks, and a missing `role="navigation"` means this page's nav region is not discoverable by keyboard users who jump between landmarks. It passes the visual check but fails landmark navigation.

**Fix:** Add `role="navigation"` to the div in for-agencies.html.

### 9. Comparison tables at 320px have no scroll affordance [BOTH]

**File:** `web/pricing.html` `.price-table-wrap`, `web/vs-microsoft-agt.html` `.table-wrap`

Both table wrappers use `overflow-x: auto` with `min-width: 640px` (pricing) and `min-width: 480px` (vs-microsoft-agt) on the table. At 320px, both tables scroll horizontally. The scroll works, but there is no visual indicator that the table continues beyond the viewport: no right-edge gradient fade, no scroll shadow, no "scroll right" hint. At 320px the table header row is visible but the right-side columns (which contain the key differentiation data) are hidden with no indication they exist.

This is the most content-critical point in the user journey for anyone evaluating plans on mobile - they may read "Free" and "Builder" columns but miss "Team" entirely.

**Fix:** Add a right-edge gradient overlay to `.price-table-wrap` and `.table-wrap`:
```css
.price-table-wrap { position: relative; }
.price-table-wrap::after {
  content: '';
  position: absolute;
  top: 0; right: 0;
  width: 2.5rem; height: 100%;
  background: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, var(--bg));
  pointer-events: none;
}
```
Fade the overlay out once the user has scrolled to the end (JS or scroll-driven animation).

### 10. JSON-LD dateModified stale, and Builder description omits anchoring [LOCAL]

**File:** `web/index.html:120` (dateModified), `web/index.html:116` (Builder offer description)

The SoftwareApplication JSON-LD says `"dateModified": "2026-08-04"`. Today is 2026-08-18 and the Builder plan gained two new features (anchoring, public anchor receipt). The Offer description for Builder reads `"description": "500k events/month, RFC 3161 trusted timestamps, shareable client proof links and live badge"` - missing both new features.

Search engines use dateModified to prioritize crawling. Stale dates suppress re-crawl for changed pages. The incomplete Builder description means the new anchor features cannot appear in LLM-indexed answers or rich snippets.

**Fix:** Update `dateModified` to `"2026-08-18"` and add the anchoring features to the Builder offer description.

## Secondary findings - grouped by severity

### HIGH (fix before deploy of new pages)

**S1. for-agencies page: no value-prop or pricing context above the fold on mobile.**
At 375px, the above-the-fold content is: eyebrow "For freelancers and agencies", headline "Give your client a receipt they can verify without trusting you", lede text, and two CTAs ("See a live verified record", "Get started in five minutes"). The CTAs go to /verify?demo and /start - not to /pricing. There is no price signal, no "free to start" copy, and no mention of cost until section 8 of a long page. For a conversion-oriented page this is a missed first-fold opportunity. The /pricing CTA should appear near the top with a price anchor ("Free to start, $29 for trusted timestamps").

**S2. vs-microsoft-agt SVG trust-boundary diagram not annotated for screen readers at 320px.**
The SVG has `role="img"` with `aria-labelledby` referencing a `<title>` and `<desc>` element, which is correct. However, at 320px the SVG scales to about 320px wide from a `viewBox="0 0 700 280"`. The text inside the SVG (font-size 10-12px in SVG coordinates) renders at approximately 10 * (320/700) = 4.6px in the browser - illegible. The diagram becomes decoration without the text. A screen-reader user gets the description; a sighted user at 320px gets an image with unreadable labels.

**Fix:** Add a text fallback table below the SVG, visible only on narrow viewports (`@media (max-width: 480px)`), or rewrite the SVG labels as foreignObject elements that scale with the page.

**S3. For-agencies page has no footer pricing CTA.**
The homepage ends its pricing section with a "Start free" button in the footer nav section. The for-agencies page ends with a FAQ section and a small "back to home" link. A user who has read the entire page and is persuaded has to navigate away to convert. The page needs a conversion section near the bottom: plan cards (or a link to them) with a primary CTA.

### MEDIUM

**M1. compare.html says "Last updated 5 August 2026" but compare content references features that may have changed.**
`web/compare.html` has a visible "Last updated 5 August 2026" date and a comparison table. This is only 13 days old - not a live problem, but any future update to the comparison table should also update this date. It's a maintenance trap.

**M2. Hero secondary CTA text length imbalance.**
On the production homepage the primary CTA reads "Guard my agent" (3 words, short) and the secondary reads "Verify a record yourself" (4 words, longer). The buttons render at different widths with mismatched padding. At 375px they stack vertically which resolves it, but at 1280px they sit side by side and the secondary is noticeably wider. The secondary button text could be shortened to "Verify a record" without losing meaning.

**M3. Code blocks in for-agencies.html and vs-microsoft-agt.html lack copy buttons.**
The homepage code block has a `.copy-btn` with clipboard functionality. The `for-agencies.html` page has multiple `<pre>` blocks (quickstart commands, export commands) and `vs-microsoft-agt.html` has none. These are the pages most likely to be used by someone following along; copy buttons save a friction step that matters for install rates.

**M4. The `/verify` page upload drop zone says "Drop your own bundle.json here" but is not a real file input on iOS Safari.**
The drop zone uses drag-and-drop events. On iOS there is no native file drag; the "tap to choose a file" fallback exists, but the primary label still says "Drop". At 375px the word "Drop" is misleading. The label should be "Choose a file to verify" or use conditional text ("Tap to choose a file" on touch, "Drop bundle.json here" on desktop) via JS.

**M5. The "Start free" nav button on index.html links to `/#pricing` (the in-page pricing section) rather than `/pricing` (the dedicated pricing page).**
All other pages point to `/pricing`. On the homepage, `/#pricing` scrolls to the in-page plan cards, which is reasonable. But the inconsistency means the homepage is the only page where "Start free" does not navigate to the dedicated page. If the in-page section and the /pricing page ever diverge (they currently do on the Builder feature list), the homepage sends users to the shorter version.

**M6. docs.html `pr --version` warning.**
`web/docs.html:460` notes that `pr --version` may answer `pr (GNU coreutils) 8.32` on some systems. This is a real gotcha (the GNU coreutils `pr` command shadows the Provenrail CLI). The warning is present but buried in the troubleshooting section. It should be a visible callout earlier in the install step.

### LOW

**L1. No `font-optical-sizing: auto` on display headings.**
Covered in #6 above but worth calling out separately - the hero headline at 4.6rem would visually improve with optical sizing. This is a one-line CSS addition.

**L2. `.nav-links > a` on non-homepage pages still includes `/#how-it-works` etc.**
When a user on /docs clicks "How it works", they navigate to the homepage and scroll. This is not broken but reads as undesigned. These anchor links have no semantic value on inner pages.

**L3. The footer tagline is identical across all pages.**
"Off-box, hash-chained, independently verifiable records of what your AI agent did. Designed for audits and incident reconstruction." This is fine, and consistent. Not a problem.

**L4. Comparison table in vs-microsoft-agt.html has `text-align: left` overrides on TD cells for non-centered columns, applying both inline style and class styling.**
`thead th:nth-child(2)` and `thead th:nth-child(3)` are styled via class, but `td:first-child` gets `.feat` class and additional inline color. This works but is fragile - adding a column would require updating the nth-child selectors. Low priority, low risk.

**L5. The production homepage shows light mode by default.**
Screenshots confirm the site is rendering in light mode (cream/tan background). The dark mode is defined in the CSS and should work with `prefers-color-scheme: dark`. The light mode palette is coherent and intentional. No issue here - just confirming both modes are defined and the production site correctly renders both.

## Technical: no breakage found

- Production console: zero errors on homepage, /pricing, /compare, /start, /verify, /verify?tamper.
- Local console: only `POST /pv 501` which is the analytics beacon against Python's dev server - not a real error.
- /verify?demo and /verify?tamper both work correctly on production. The tamper path shows "Tampering detected" with specific chain-break diagnostics. The demo path shows "Bundle verified / Verified and witnessed". Both complete without console errors.
- Font loading: DM Sans and DM Mono are preloaded with `<link rel="preload">` and `font-display: swap`. No FOUT observed.
- Skip link is present on all pages. Focus styles use `var(--ring)` (double ring, accent color). Visible on keyboard navigation.
- The pricing comparison table wraps in `overflow-x: auto` on all breakpoints. Content does not overflow the body on any tested size.
- 404 page exists and is styled correctly.

## Copy pass: clean

No em-dashes, en-dashes, or Unicode curly quotes found in any content HTML. No AI slop terms (delve, moreover, leverage, utilize, in today's landscape, it's worth noting) found. No three-item triads in hero or section copy. The honest-scope callouts ("Provenrail detects tampering of records that were written; it cannot force an uncooperative agent to write in the first place") are present and clearly worded on every page that makes a tamper-evidence claim. The copy overall reads as direct, specific, and technically credible. The one exception is the start.html contradiction documented in #1 above.
